Hams Al Rawaya for Contracting Est.

10+ Years Experience

Hams Al Rawaya For General Construction Est. is a Saudi-based equipment rental and general construction services company committed to supporting contractors, project owners, and industrial businesses with reliable equipment and responsive rental solutions. We provide a practical fleet of construction and access equipment designed to meet the demanding requirements of construction, infrastructure, industrial, and maintenance projects across Saudi Arabia.

Our equipment rental portfolio includes Generators, Aerial Work Platforms, Boom Lifts, Scissor Lifts, Telehandlers, Forklifts, and other specialized construction equipment, enabling our clients to access the right machinery without the cost and complexity of ownership.

We focus on equipment quality, operational safety, availability, and dependable service. Our machines are selected to deliver efficient performance across diverse working environments, while our professional team ensures responsive support throughout the rental period.

At Hams Al Rawaya, we understand that equipment availability directly impacts project productivity. That is why we strive to provide flexible rental solutions, well-maintained equipment, timely delivery, and dedicated customer support helping our clients keep their projects moving safely, efficiently, and on schedule.

Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

What types of equipment do you offer for rent?
We provide a comprehensive range of construction, industrial, and access machinery across Saudi Arabia.
Our fleet includes:

Aerial Work Platforms (AWP): Electric & diesel scissor lifts, articulated boom lifts, and telescopic stick booms.

Material Handling: Forklifts, telehandlers, boom trucks, and mobile cranes.

Power & Air Solutions: Silent/canopied diesel generators (30 kVA to 2,000 kVA), load banks, and industrial air compressors.

Site Support & Logistics: Lighting towers, portable cabins, storage containers, transport vehicles, lifting accessories, and safety products (PPE).
Do you offer both short-term and long-term rental agreements?
Yes. We provide flexible rental solutions tailored to your project timeline, whether you require machinery for short-duration tasks or long-term industrial and infrastructure projects.
Which regions in Saudi Arabia do you serve?
We serve clients across all regions of Riyadh, supporting sectors ranging from commercial construction and infrastructure to defense, manufacturing, and oil & gas. Our central office is located in Riyadh.
Do rental units include certified operators?
Yes. Our cranes, boom trucks, forklifts, and powered access equipment can be supplied with experienced, safety-trained operators to ensure safe and efficient operation on your job site.
Are your lifting and material handling machines certified?
All our cranes, boom trucks, and forklifts come complete with Load Test Certificates and meet all relevant Saudi statutory and safety compliance standards.
What working heights are available for your Aerial Work Platforms (AWP)?
Scissor Lifts: Working heights ranging from 7.8 meters up to 22 meters, available in electric, bi-energy, and rough-terrain 4WD diesel models.

Boom Lifts: Articulated and telescopic booms offering working heights from 10.89 meters up to 48 meters+ (with outreach options reaching over 24 meters).
What capacities of power generators and air compressors do you provide?
Generators: From 30 kVA up to 2,000 kVA, including silent, canopied, and containerized units, complete with ATS panels, distribution cables, and auxiliary fuel tanks.

Air Compressors: Capacities from 185 CFM to 1,025 CFM (pressure ratings from 100 to 175 PSI / 7 to 12 Bar), available with or without aftercoolers.
What lifting capacities do your cranes and forklifts handle?
Forklifts: 40+ units starting from 3.5 tons, including counterbalanced, rough terrain, side loader, and reach trucks.

Boom Trucks: Ranging from 4.5 to 15 tons.

Mobile Cranes: Heavy-duty cranes ranging from 50 to 220 tons.
